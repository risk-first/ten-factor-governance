# ISO/IEC 27001 Gap Analysis

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 defines requirements for an Information Security Management System (ISMS). ISO/IEC 27005:2022 elaborates the risk-management lifecycle underneath Clauses 6.1 and 8. [Ten Factor Governance](/docs/factors/) is an architectural model for making governance operable.

They are complementary rather than interchangeable: Ten Factor aligns strongly with the operational spine—requirement → risk → control → policy → evaluation → evidence → enforcement → audit—while the outer management-system envelope (context, leadership, objectives, competence, Statement of Applicability, management review, CAPA) and richer ISO/IEC 27005 risk-assessment semantics remain the largest gaps. Proposed management-system objects are stubbed under [ISO/IEC 27001 Definitions](/docs/artifacts/definitions/).

**Coverage labels:** 
 - **Full** = direct corresponding capability (not by itself ISO conformity); 
 - **Partial** = useful machinery exists but an ISO-required element is missing; 
 - **Conceptual** = the principle or schema could host the concern but has no first-class semantics for it; 
 - **Does not cover** / **Not represented** = no material direct representation. Encoding an Annex A topic as a [Control](/docs/artifacts/definitions/assess/control) alone is **Conceptual**, not Full—structured assets, schedules, tests and evidence are what move coverage up.

Requirement descriptions below are concise paraphrases of public ISO material, not a substitute for a licensed copy of the standard.

## Synthesis

Across clauses and Annex A, **Ten Factor often has the right architectural principle but not the domain object**, and it lacks the management-system and asset semantics ISO expects. The framework should not reimplement all 93 Annex A domains as core schemas—cryptography, malware protection, web filtering, physical perimeters and screening belong as ISO-derived control/catalogue content plus integrations to specialist evidence sources.

ISO/IEC 27001/27005 validates much of the architectural direction behind Ten Factor. The strongest matches are activity/risk focus, Governance Twins binding guidance–risk–control–policy to implementation, versioned change, continuous evidence and assurance, dependency governance, and ownership/[Party Register](/docs/artifacts/definitions/cross-cutting/party-register).

The substantive omissions are the organisation governing governance—context and interested parties; ISMS scope; leadership and authority; objectives and metrics; competence and communication; formal applicability (SoA); management review; and complete corrective-action/continual-improvement records—plus richer ISO/IEC 27005 assessment and treatment semantics.

The right response is **extension, not redesign**. Highest-value generic additions, now stubbed as [definitions](/docs/artifacts/definitions/):

- [**GovernanceContext**](/docs/artifacts/definitions/define/organisation-context) / [**GovernanceScope**](/docs/artifacts/definitions/define/aims-scope)
- [**GovernedResourceCatalog**](/docs/artifacts/definitions/operate/governed-resource)
- richer [**RiskAssessment**](/docs/artifacts/definitions/decide/risk-assessment) (criteria, method, scenario, consequence, likelihood, residual)
- [**ApplicabilityProfile**](/docs/artifacts/definitions/assess/applicability-profile) (Statement of Applicability)
- [**ObjectiveMetric**](/docs/artifacts/definitions/define/management-objective)
- [**ManagementReview**](/docs/artifacts/definitions/evaluate/management-review)
- [**CorrectiveAction**](/docs/artifacts/definitions/enforce/corrective-action)

[**CompetenceRecord**](/docs/artifacts/definitions/cross-cutting/competence-record), [**CommunicationPlan**](/docs/artifacts/definitions/cross-cutting/communication-requirement), [**Incident**](/docs/artifacts/definitions/operate/incident), supplier and business-continuity structures are useful as profiles or external referenced schemas rather than core primitives. Ten Factor needs principally clarifications—management-system activities, evidence lifecycle, management feedback, obligation dependencies and differentiated ownership (risk owner vs treatment owner vs residual-risk acceptance authority vs auditor)—not an eleventh factor.

The intended division of labour:

- **ISO/IEC 27001** — what organisational information-security management system must exist
- **ISO/IEC 27005** — how its risk-management machinery should work
- **[Ten Factor Governance](/docs/factors/)** — what architectural properties make that governance operable
