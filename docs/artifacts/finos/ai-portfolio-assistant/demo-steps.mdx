# AI Portfolio Assistant Demo

1. **Select a portfolio**
   - The Portfolio app broadcasts an `fdc3.portfolio` context.
   - The AI Assistant receives the selected portfolio.

2. **Analyse portfolio risk**
   - The user asks: *“What are the biggest risks in this portfolio?”*
   - The AI Assistant raises `AnalyzePortfolioRisk`.
   - The Desktop Agent intercepts the intent and checks it with the Governance Gateway.
   - Governance returns **ALLOW**.
   - The Risk app performs the deterministic analysis and returns the result.
   - The AI Assistant explains the result to the user.

3. **Investigate a specific risk**
   - The AI Assistant raises `ViewAnalysis`, `ViewNews`, or a similar research intent for an affected `fdc3.instrument`.
   - Governance returns **ALLOW**.
   - The Research app provides supporting information.
   - The AI combines the risk and research results into an explanation.

4. **Request an investment recommendation**
   - The user asks: *“What should I do about the energy concentration?”*
   - The AI Assistant raises `CreatePortfolioRecommendation`.
   - Governance evaluates applicable controls, such as:
     - approved AI model;
     - current portfolio data;
     - valid risk analysis;
     - permitted recommendation capability.
   - Governance returns **ALLOW** or **CONDITIONAL ALLOW**.
   - The AI produces a recommendation.

5. **Create a rebalance proposal**
   - The user says: *“Reduce energy exposure to 15%.”*
   - The AI Assistant raises `CreateRebalanceProposal`.
   - Governance verifies that the AI is permitted to construct, but not execute, a proposed trade.
   - The portfolio/rebalancing app produces a proposed `fdc3.orderList`.

6. **Send the proposal for human review**
   - The AI Assistant raises `ReviewOrders` with the proposed order list.
   - Governance returns **ALLOW — HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED**.
   - The Desktop Agent forwards the request to the Order Entry app.
   - The user can inspect the proposed trades.

7. **Attempt autonomous execution**
   - The AI attempts to raise `ExecuteOrders`.
   - The Desktop Agent again checks with the Governance Gateway.
   - Governance determines that the AI has no delegated trade-execution authority.
   - Governance returns **DENY**.
   - The Desktop Agent does not forward the intent.

8. **Inspect the governance decision**
   - The Governance app displays the decision and its traceability:
     - governed activity;
     - actor;
     - applicable policy/control;
     - evaluation results;
     - evidence;
     - decision;
     - required human action.
   - The denied execution demonstrates that governance changes system behaviour rather than merely recording it.

9. **Connect the runtime decision to ISO 42001**
   - Show how the controls and evidence relate back to the organisation's AI governance requirements.
   - Demonstrate the chain from:
     - ISO 42001 guidance;
     - risk;
     - control/policy;
     - sensitive activity;
     - evaluation;
     - evidence;
     - runtime decision.

10. **Show the authority progression**
    - Summarise the increasing level of delegated authority:

      `observe → analyse → explain → recommend → propose → execute`

    - The key point: **Ten-Factor governance determines how far the AI may progress along that chain, based on explicit, machine-readable governance.**

11. **Opportunities for further expansion**
     - Here, we are just showing how we can use FDC3 to govern an AI application.
     - We could apply the same governance gateway to MCP.
     - Also, the governance runtime itself could be an AI agent running to observe and constrain the assistant.
