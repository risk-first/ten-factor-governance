# AI Portfolio Assistant

Consider a financial institution providing portfolio managers with an **AI
Portfolio Assistant** operating across a suite of FDC3 applications. The
assistant can access the user's portfolio, invoke specialist applications to
calculate exposures and stress scenarios, retrieve relevant investment research,
and explain the results. Rather than asking the AI to perform financial
calculations itself, deterministic applications provide portfolio, risk and
market information, while the AI combines and interprets those results to help
the portfolio manager understand their positions and risks.

As the assistant becomes more capable, however, the consequences of its actions
increase. Reading a portfolio or explaining a risk may require relatively little
authority, while recommending that the portfolio manager reduce a position is
more consequential. Constructing a proposed set of orders goes further still,
and actually submitting those orders would give the AI direct authority over the
portfolio. Governance therefore needs to control **how much authority is
delegated to the AI and under what conditions**. An organisation might allow the
assistant to analyse and explain autonomously, permit recommendations only when
specified controls have been satisfied, require human approval for proposed
orders, and prohibit autonomous trade execution entirely.

This example follows that progression from **observe → analyse → explain →
recommend → propose → execute**. It demonstrates how Ten-Factor Governance can
make the boundaries around AI authority explicit, machine-readable, enforceable
and auditable across a real multi-application workflow. The example also shows
how those governance mechanisms can form part of an ISO/IEC 42001-aligned AI
management system.

Controls are intended to stack by layer:
 - [CCC Kubernetes](https://ccc.finos.org/catalogs/orchestration/k8s) on the shared Managed Kubernetes host (both K8s deployments)
 - [CCC Multi-Agent Reference Architecture](https://ccc.finos.org/catalogs/ai-ml/multi-agent-refarch) capabilities and controls composed into the [AI Portfolio Assistant](./gemara/components/ai-portfolio-assistant/) twin
 - [CCC Object Storage](../ccc-obj-stor/) for the Decisions and Evidence Record bucket
 - [FINOS AI Governance Framework](../ai-governance/) controls in the shared [Governance Model](./gemara/) (lexicon → policy), hosted by the [Artifact Repository](./gemara/components/artifact-repository/) twin
 - Runtime evaluation and evidence behaviours in the [Governance Runtime](./gemara/components/governance-runtime/) twin
 - The [ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS](./iso/aims) documents wrap that combined set in a management-system envelope

See [deployment](./deployment) for the C4 view and twin links, and [Gemara component twins](./gemara/) for the catalog tree.
