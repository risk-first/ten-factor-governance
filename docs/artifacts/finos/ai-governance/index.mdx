# FINOS AI Governance Framework

The [FINOS AI Governance Framework](https://air-governance-framework.finos.org)
publishes a structured set of AI risks, mitigations, and underlying principles
for financial-services use cases. In this site, those materials are rendered as
three Gemara catalogs:

- [Guidance Catalog](./guidance-catalog) for mitigations and implementation guidance
- [Principles Catalog](./principles-catalog) for the framework's core principles
- [Vectors Catalog](./vectors-catalog) for its AI risk vectors

The catalogs are sourced from the FINOS AIGF artifacts under
`src/data/governance/finos/aigf/` and rendered directly for browsing,
cross-reference, and reuse in downstream governance content.
