# FINOS AIGF Guidance Catalog

This page renders the FINOS AI Governance Framework's mitigation catalog as a
[Gemara](/docs/artifacts/gemara) `GuidanceCatalog`. It is the implementation
guidance layer of the framework: grouped mitigations, detailed statements, and
recommendations, with links out to the AIGF principles and risk vectors they
support.

For the companion catalogs, see the
[Principles Catalog](./principles-catalog) and
[Vectors Catalog](./vectors-catalog).
