# ISO/IEC 27001 Artifacts

Everything an ISMS needs from the [artifact catalogue](/docs/artifacts/definitions/),
under the name [ISO/IEC 27001](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001) gives it —
including the ISO/IEC 27005 risk overlay.

Some of these are management-system objects the standard adds; others are
artifacts it shares with Gemara under a different name. Information Security
Risk is the Risk catalogue, Interested Party is the Party Register. Follow a
term to the artifact that holds it.

## How the artifacts connect

Context frames the scope, the resources in scope are assessed for risk, the
Statement of Applicability selects the controls policy imports, and reviews and
incidents raise corrective action.
