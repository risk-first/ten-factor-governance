<GovernanceArtifact>

<Purpose>

Recurring internal audit of the AIMS: coverage cycle, frequency per audit type, auditor independence and follow-up.

</Purpose>

<Role>

ISO/IEC 42001 Clause 9.2 requires an internal audit programme. Gemara [Audit Log](/docs/artifacts/definitions/assure/audit-log) records an audit that happened; this artefact is the programme that plans recurring audits by **frequency** (for example annual conformity, quarterly control sampling), staffs them and follows findings. Calendar windows for a single occurrence belong on the audit log or corrective-action trail, not as the programme's primary schedule.

</Role>
