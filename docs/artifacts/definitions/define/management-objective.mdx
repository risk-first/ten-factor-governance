<Purpose>

A measurable management-system objective with target, measure, owner, period and result.

</Purpose>

<Role>

ISO/IEC 27001 Clauses 6.2 and 9.1 and ISO/IEC 42001 Clauses 6.2 and 9.1 require objectives and monitoring of their achievement. Ten Factor evaluates against policy and controls; it does not yet bind those results to stated ISMS or AIMS objectives. ISO/IEC 27001 names this Objective Metric.

This page is a stub: fields, examples and schema are still to be specified.

</Role>
