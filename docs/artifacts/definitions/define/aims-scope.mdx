<Purpose>

Organisational and technical boundaries of the ISMS or AIMS, including systems, activities, sites, suppliers and exclusions.

</Purpose>

<Role>

ISO/IEC 27001 and 42001 Clause 4.3 require determining the boundaries and applicability of the management system. A Governance Twin is scoped to a sensitive activity; this artefact records the enterprise (or programme) boundary those twins sit inside. ISO/IEC 27001 names this Governance Scope.

This page is a stub: fields, examples and schema are still to be specified.

</Role>
