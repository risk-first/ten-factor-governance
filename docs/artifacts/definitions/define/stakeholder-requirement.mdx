<GovernanceArtifact>

<Purpose>

A requirement an interested party places on the management system, with provenance into guidance, control, risk and policy.

</Purpose>

<Role>

ISO/IEC 27001 and 42001 Clause 4.2 require determining which interested-party requirements are relevant. Ten Factor currently hosts those as guidance or policy text without a first-class source link. ISO/IEC 27001 names this concern Obligation; ISO/IEC 42001 names it Stakeholder Requirement.

This page is a stub: fields, examples and schema are still to be specified.

</Role>
