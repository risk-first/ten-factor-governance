<Purpose>

Statement of which controls apply, are excluded, or are treated—the Statement of Applicability.

</Purpose>

<Role>

ISO/IEC 27001 Clause 6.1.3 and ISO/IEC 42001 Clause 6.1.3 require a Statement of Applicability: which Annex A controls are applicable, why, how they are implemented or excluded, and residual risk accepted. Gemara can list controls but not a formal applicability determination.

This page is a stub: fields, examples and schema are still to be specified.

</Role>
