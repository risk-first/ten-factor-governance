<GovernanceArtifact>

<Purpose>

Actions that might introduce risk—the hinge between definition layers and
measurement layers.

</Purpose>

<Role>

Layer 4 names the activity being governed. Definitions above (guidance,
controls, policy) describe what good looks like; measurements below
(evaluation, enforcement, audit) inspect instances of the activity. In Ten
Factor terms, this is where Factor I’s sensitive activity meets Factor II’s
Governance Twin: the activity is the shared identity of implementation and
governance.

</Role>

<Examples>

<RevealItem title="Employee FOSS contribution" defaultOpen>

Writing open source software and attempting to contribute it from within the
organisation—across company-moderated, company-interest and personal contexts.
Catalogs and logs organise around that activity: registration, review, merge
gates and quarterly audit all refer to contribution instances, not to “GitHub”
in the abstract.

**Source:** Worked FOSS contribution activity in the [FOSS Contribution Policy](https://github.com/robmoffat/FOSS-Contribution-Gemara/blob/main/policy.yaml) programme (`foss-contribution-policy`).

</RevealItem>

<RevealItem title="Mortgage approval in a lending platform">

Approving or declining a mortgage application is the sensitive activity. The
Governance Twin binds fair-lending guidance, controls and policy to that
decision; evaluation and enforcement attach to each approval instance.

**Source:** Worked illustration in [Build a Governance Twin](/docs/factors/build-a-governance-twin) (Approve Mortgage).

</RevealItem>

<RevealItem title="AI-generated credit recommendation">

Producing a credit recommendation from a model is the activity. Principles such
as least exposure and auditability, plus bias and explainability controls,
govern that recommendation—not the ML platform as a whole.

**Source:** Worked illustration in [Build a Governance Twin](/docs/factors/build-a-governance-twin), oriented by [AIR-PRIN](https://grc.store/finos-aigf/air-prin/versions/0.2.0) principles such as *Least Exposure* and *Auditability*.

</RevealItem>

</Examples>

<AntiPatterns>

<RevealItem title="Naming a platform instead of an activity">

“Lending platform” or “AI stack” as the Layer 4 object. You cannot say which
decision was governed.

</RevealItem>

<RevealItem title="Activity with no definition above it">

A named activity that no guidance, control or policy attaches to. Measurement
then has nothing to evaluate against.

</RevealItem>

</AntiPatterns>
