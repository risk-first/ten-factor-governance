<Purpose>

What is communicated about the management system, to whom, when, through which channel, and with what evidence.

</Purpose>

<Role>

ISO/IEC 27001 and 42001 Clause 7.4 require determining internal and external communications relevant to the management system. ISO/IEC 27001 names this Communication Plan. Incident communication and reporting obligations specialise this object.

This page is a stub: fields, examples and schema are still to be specified.
