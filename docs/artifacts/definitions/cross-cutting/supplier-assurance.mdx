<Purpose>

Assurance over a supplier contributing AI systems, services or resources: obligations, attestations, versions, risk and evidence.

</Purpose>

<Role>

ISO/IEC 42001 Annex A.10.3 requires appropriate governance for suppliers contributing AI systems, services or resources. [Govern Dependencies](/docs/factors/govern-dependencies) is the architectural principle; this profile is the missing domain object. ISO/IEC 27001 treats suppliers as a profile under Governed Resource rather than a first-class type here.

This page is a stub: fields, examples and schema are still to be specified.

</Role>
