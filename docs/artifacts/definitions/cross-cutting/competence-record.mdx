<Purpose>

Evidence that a role has the required skill, qualification and training, with expiry.

</Purpose>

<Role>

ISO/IEC 27001 and 42001 Clause 7.2 require determining necessary competence, ensuring people are competent, and retaining evidence. Ten Factor names owners; it does not record the competence that makes that ownership credible.

This page is a stub: fields, examples and schema are still to be specified.

</Role>
