<Purpose>

How top management reviews the management system: frequency, attendees, required inputs and required outputs.

</Purpose>

<Role>

ISO/IEC 27001 and 42001 Clause 9.3 require top management to review the management system at planned intervals. This artefact is the **programme** for that process — who chairs, who attends, what every review must consider and what it must produce — defined by **frequency**, not by the minutes of one sitting. Occurrence results (findings, decisions, actions) belong with review evidence or corrective-action follow-up, analogous to how [Audit Programme](/docs/artifacts/definitions/assure/audit-programme) plans audits while Audit Log records an audit that happened.

</Role>
