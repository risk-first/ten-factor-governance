<GovernanceArtifact>

<Purpose>

A recorded review of the management system by top management: inputs, decisions and resulting actions.

</Purpose>

<Role>

ISO/IEC 27001 and 42001 Clause 9.3 require top management to review the management system at planned intervals. Together with CAPA, this is the largest management-system structural hole against Ten Factor. Audit logs review programme conformance; they are not a management-review minute.

This page is a stub: fields, examples and schema are still to be specified.

</Role>
