<Purpose>

A versioned assessment of risk: criteria, method, likelihood, consequence, residual and review trigger.

</Purpose>

<Role>

ISO/IEC 27001 Clauses 6.1 and 8 (with ISO/IEC 27005) and ISO/IEC 42001 Clauses 6.1 and 8.2 require a repeatable risk-assessment process. Gemara [Risk](/docs/artifacts/definitions/decide/risk) catalogues name exposures; they do not yet record the assessment instance that produced them, including AI impact-assessment inputs.

This page is a stub: fields, examples and schema are still to be specified.

</Role>
