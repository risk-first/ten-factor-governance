# Aspects of Good Governance

Empirical work on organisational controls, capture and enforcement—including board-independence meta-analyses, whistleblower and enforcement studies, and major corporate-failure cases—suggests that governance acts as a brake only under specific institutional conditions. 


| Governance dimension | Regime favouring restraint | Regime favouring misconduct | Related factors | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Observability** | Independent access to raw information; unpredictable or real-world testing | Management controls data; predictable tests; hidden actions | [Evidence by Default](/docs/factors/evidence-by-default); [Continuous Governance](/docs/factors/continuous-governance); [Build a Governance Twin](/docs/factors/build-a-governance-twin) | [Volkswagen](https://www.epa.gov/enforcement/learn-about-volkswagen-violations) shows the vulnerability of predictable compliance testing. |
| **Escalation** | Multiple protected routes directly to independent decision-makers | Hierarchical filtering, retaliation, departmental silos | [Governance Has Owners](/docs/factors/governance-has-owners); [Context In, Decisions Out](/docs/factors/context-in-decisions-out); [Continuous Governance](/docs/factors/continuous-governance) | [Wells Fargo](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/72971/000119312517118654/d375947ddefa14a.htm); [whistleblower evidence](https://doi.org/10.1111/1475-679X.12303). |
| **Monitor independence** | Financial, career and informational independence | Consulting conflicts, dependence on management, collusion | [Build a Governance Twin](/docs/factors/build-a-governance-twin); [Evidence by Default](/docs/factors/evidence-by-default); [Govern the Factory and the Product Separately](/docs/factors/govern-factory-and-product-separately) | [Enron](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CPRT-107SPRT80393/pdf/CPRT-107SPRT80393.pdf); [board-independence meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1177/0149206318801999). |
| **Authority** | Monitor can stop activity, remove leaders or impose binding constraints | Advice can be ignored without consequence | [Context In, Decisions Out](/docs/factors/context-in-decisions-out); [Continuous Governance](/docs/factors/continuous-governance); [Governance Has Owners](/docs/factors/governance-has-owners) | [Federal Reserve's Wells Fargo growth restriction](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/enforcement20180202a.htm). |
| **Sanction credibility** | Potential offenders expect meaningful investigation and consequence | Enforcement remote, delayed or unlikely | [Continuous Governance](/docs/factors/continuous-governance); [Evidence by Default](/docs/factors/evidence-by-default); [Governance Has Owners](/docs/factors/governance-has-owners) | [SEC-enforcement study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacceco.2011.01.004); [DOJ intervention study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacceco.2020.101357). |
| **Capacity and competence** | Resources/expertise scale with organisational complexity | Workload, technical complexity or fragmented jurisdiction overwhelms monitor | [Governance Has Owners](/docs/factors/governance-has-owners); [Build a Governance Twin](/docs/factors/build-a-governance-twin); [Governance Is Composable](/docs/factors/governance-is-composable); [Continuous Governance](/docs/factors/continuous-governance) | [Wirecard](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-identifies-deficiencies-in-german-supervision-wirecard%E2%80%99s-financial); [inspection ABMs](https://doi.org/10.7906/indecs.14.3.1). |
| **Organisational culture** | Challenge is legitimate and reporting is normal | Performance pressure rationalises rule breaking | [Evidence by Default](/docs/factors/evidence-by-default); [Continuous Governance](/docs/factors/continuous-governance); [Governance Has Owners](/docs/factors/governance-has-owners) | [Wells Fargo](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/72971/000119312517118654/d375947ddefa14a.htm); [normalisation theory](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0191-3085(03)25001-2). |
| **Redundancy** | Internal audit, board, staff, external auditors/regulators and outsiders have partially independent channels | Single gatekeeper is a common point of failure | [Continuous Governance](/docs/factors/continuous-governance); [Evidence by Default](/docs/factors/evidence-by-default); [Govern Dependencies](/docs/factors/govern-dependencies); [Governance Is Composable](/docs/factors/governance-is-composable) | [Distributed detection in Dyck et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2010.01614.x); [Enron's lesson about correlated gatekeeper failure](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CPRT-107SPRT82147/html/CPRT-107SPRT82147.htm). |
| **Institutional environment** | External rule of law and low ambient corruption reinforce independent monitors | Surrounding institutions themselves tolerate capture | [Governance Is Composable](/docs/factors/governance-is-composable); [Govern Dependencies](/docs/factors/govern-dependencies); [Governance Has Owners](/docs/factors/governance-has-owners) | [Board-independence effect is stronger in less-corrupt national environments](https://doi.org/10.1177/0149206318801999). |
