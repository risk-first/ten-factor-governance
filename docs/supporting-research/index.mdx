# Supporting Research

This section collects background analysis that situates Ten Factor Governance in a wider
governance and standards landscape. The factors and artifacts describe how to make
governance operable; the notes here examine *why* those properties matter and how they
map onto established requirements.

That includes empirical lessons about when organisational controls actually restrain
misconduct, and comparative mappings against management-system standards such as
ISO/IEC 42001—where Ten Factor and Gemara align, where they leave gaps, and what
profiles or artefacts would close them.
