# 17 — How it decides

## Say

- Gemara is the schema. Three catalogs, joined.
- Capabilities: what the system can do. That is the attack surface.
- Threats: what can go wrong on those capabilities.
- Controls: what we require so those threats do not land.
- The next three slides are one thread through that chain.

## Don't put on the slide

- YAML. Specific ids. Policy. Risks. The boxes are the map.
