Corporate governance vs AI governance

“The principal–agent problem refers to the conflict in interests and priorities that arises when one person or entity (the agent) takes actions on behalf of another person or entity (the principal).”

  • AI governance is a new face of that dilemma.
  • Intelligence ≠ authority. Organisations already govern agents who may know more than their principals.
  • Governance constrains power, not intelligence — through delegated authority, separation of duties, approvals and accountability.

Wikipedia. Principal–agent problem. Jensen, M. C. & Meckling, W. H. (1976). Theory of the Firm.
