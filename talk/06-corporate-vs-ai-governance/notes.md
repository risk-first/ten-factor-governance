# 06 — Corporate vs AI governance

## Say

- Read the Wikipedia definition, then name the parties: the principal wants something done; the agent does it on their behalf.
- The dilemma is conflict of interest plus asymmetric information. Wikipedia’s next sentence: the problem worsens when interests and information diverge, and when the principal cannot punish the agent.
- Our industry already governs agents who may know more than their principals: directors, traders, credit officers, model risk.
- An AI agent that can write the order is still just an agent. Intelligence is not authority. Governance constrains power through delegated authority, separation of duties, approvals and accountability.

- The rest of the Wikipedia lead (agency cost, moral hazard, adverse selection).
- A lecture on Jensen & Meckling. One name in the footer is enough.
