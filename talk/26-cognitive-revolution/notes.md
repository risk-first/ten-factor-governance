# 26 — A cognitive revolution

## Say

- Same pattern as the industrial Venn. The pie can grow. The human circle does not have to vanish.
- Subsistence cognition is the farming: retrieval, drafts, status, coordination. The thinking you do to stay alive in a knowledge economy.
- Everything else is the surplus: judgment, accountability, taste, trust, and the right to say no. That is authority, not intelligence.
- Horses had no leftover slice. Humans still do. That is why the answer is still no.
