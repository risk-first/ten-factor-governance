What good governance looks like

Clear accountability

A named owner for every sensitive activity.

Delegated authority

Power is granted, limited, and can be withdrawn.

Segregation of duties

No one proposes, approves and executes.

Independent oversight

Second line does not mark its own homework.

Risk-based controls

Effort follows material risk, not paperwork volume.

Transparency and audit

Decisions leave a trail that someone else can read.

Continuous enforcement

The brake works at runtime, not only at year-end.
