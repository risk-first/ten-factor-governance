# 24 — Software engineering isn't dead

## Say

- Return to the Hacker News question. The job is not dead. The part that was typing is cheaper.
- Software engineering is risk management. Choosing what to build, what to stop, what to put a human on. That is the work that remains.
- Point at the book. This is the same argument as Risk-First: practices exist to move risk, not to produce lines of code.

## Don't put on the slide

- A sales pitch for the book. The cover is the citation.

## Citations

- Moffat, R. Risk-First Software Development. https://riskfirst.org
