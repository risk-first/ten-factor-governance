# 08 — Governance as brake

## Say

- Answer question 1 first. Read the first sentence, then the “but”.
- Walk the five conditions in the second sentence: independent information, independence, escalation, capacity, enforceable authority. These are the difference between a real brake and compliance theatre.
- The principal–agent problem never goes away. Regulatory capture is what happens when these conditions fail.

- Enron, Wells Fargo, Wirecard case studies unless someone asks. They are in the supporting research.
