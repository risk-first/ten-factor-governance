Question 1 — Does governance work?

Governance is a brake

Governance does not have to be corrupt to fail.

But governance can constrain powerful or badly motivated actors when it possesses independent information, independence from the actor being monitored, credible escalation channels, sufficient technical and investigative capacity, and enforceable authority to impose costs or stop activity.

Moffat, R. Can Governance Restrain Bad Actors? tenfactorgovernance.org/docs/supporting-research/can-governance-restrain-bad-actors
